## What this model is

Mistral's models overview describes Mistral Small 4 as an open model and a hybrid model that unifies instruct, reasoning, and coding in a single efficient model. The same overview points users from the deprecated Mistral Small 3.2 entry to Mistral Small 4 as the alternative.

Mistral Small 4 sits in the “small but capable” lane, where the model should be judged against Qwen, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, GPT-4o, and Claude Sonnet on cost, capability, and deployment preference.
