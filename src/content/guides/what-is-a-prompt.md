## What is a prompt?

**A prompt is what you type to the AI.**

It can be a question.

It can be a job, such as “Make this shorter.”

It can be a short line that starts a chat.

You type. The AI writes back.

## Why the words matter

If you are vague, the AI has to guess.

If you are clear, the answer is clearer.

Here are two made-up prompts. They are not from a real chat.

<figure>
  <div class="guide-diagram hallucination">
    <div class="answer-card">
      <div class="label">Vague</div>
      <p>Tell me about dogs.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="answer-card">
      <div class="label">Clear</div>
      <p>Explain in four short sentences, for a ten-year-old, how a guide dog is trained.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <figcaption>The second one says what to do, how long, and who it is for.</figcaption>
</figure>

To make a prompt clearer:

- Say what you want
- Say who it is for
- Say how long it should be
- Say how it should look, such as a list

<div class="guide-key"><strong>The AI can only use the words you give it.</strong></div>

## What to remember

A prompt is what you type.

The AI writes back.

Clearer words get a clearer answer.

The AI is still guessing the next likely words. Guide 02 covered that. Check anything that matters.
