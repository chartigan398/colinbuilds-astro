An encounter in Athens

A conversation worth having.

Socrates was an Athenian philosopher. Much of what we know about his questioning comes through writers such as Plato. This is an invented conversation inspired by those writings, not a historical transcript.

In Plato’s Laches, proposed accounts of courage are examined and the dialogue ends without a settled definition. Our little “courage machine” makes the limits of simple rules visible. It does not measure real courage.
